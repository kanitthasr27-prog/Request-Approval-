# Glossary

**Sample** (สินค้าตัวอย่าง): A product item the company hands out for customer trial or marketing use.

**Sample Request** (คำขอเบิก): A Requester's request to take Samples out of the warehouse, subject to approval.

**Requester** (ผู้ขอ): Anyone who submits a Sample Request. Mainly sales staff, but also other departments such as Marketing.

**Approver** (ผู้อนุมัติ): An executive who can decide on a Sample Request. Today there are two, and either one alone is enough; may later be team/line heads.

**Loan** (ยืม): A Sample Request type where the Samples must be returned by a due date. The alternative type is **Give-away** (ให้ถาวร), where Samples are not returned.

**Warehouse Officer** (เจ้าหน้าที่คลัง): The person who physically hands over Samples once a request is approved and records that it was dispensed.

**Partial Dispense** (จ่ายบางส่วน): Dispensing less than the approved quantity because stock is short. The unfilled quantity stays outstanding and the request stays open until it is dispensed.

**Admin** (ผู้ดูแลระบบ): Maintains the list of Samples and which Approvers decide for which Requesters. Takes no part in approval.

**HR** (ฝ่ายบุคคล): Creates, deactivates and resets passwords of user accounts. Cannot see Sample Requests. Deactivating an account keeps its history.

**Dispense** (จ่ายสินค้า): The Warehouse Officer handing Samples to the Requester. Recorded as done; stock levels are not tracked.

**Request History** (ประวัติการเบิก): A Requester's past Sample Requests, shown to the Approver when deciding.
